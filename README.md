# ⚡ Zargham Leads CRM - Saudi Real Estate Outreach Kit

A high-performance, mobile-first CRM and client outreach application built for selling professional website development services to Saudi real estate brokerages and offices that currently do not have an active website.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **📱 Mobile-First Responsive Design:** Optimized for seamless smartphone usage on iOS and Android.
- **💬 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Links:** Pre-formatted with Zargham's personalized pitch injecting each business's name:
  > *"Hi [Business Name], I'm Zargham, a website developer. I came across your real estate company and wanted to introduce myself. I build professional websites that help real estate businesses showcase properties and make it easier for clients to enquire. Would you be open to seeing a quick example of my work?"*
- **🎯 120 Verified Leads Across 12 Key Saudi Markets:**
  - مكاتب بيع وشراء العقارات مكة المكرمة
  - مكاتب الوساطة العقارية الخبر
  - مؤسسات إدارة الأملاك الدمام
  - شركات التسويق العقاري جدة
  - مكاتب بيع وتأجير العقارات شرق الرياض
  - مؤسسات الوساطة العقارية شمال الرياض
  - مكاتب عقار الرياض
  - شركات التسويق العقاري الرياض
  - مكاتب عقار جدة
  - شركات عقارية الدمام
  - مكاتب عقار الخبر
  - مكاتب عقار الطائف
- **⚡ Priority Smart Sorting:** Unsent leads (`⏳ لم يُرسل`) stay prominently on top; sent leads drop down; newly added leads appear first.
- **📊 Live Analytics Dashboard:** Real-time counters for Total Leads, Unsent, Sent, and Replied leads.
- **📍 City & Search Filters:** Filter by city (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Al Khobar, Taif, Makkah) or search by name / phone number.
- **📄 Printable PDF Report:** Automatically generated high-resolution PDF report (`leads_report.pdf`).
- **💾 LocalStorage Persistence:** Preserves your outreach progress and status changes directly in your browser.
